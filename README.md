# NOVA

A free, console-style game launcher and social app for Windows.

## Features

- **Game library:** your games from Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox, EA, Ubisoft and local folders in one place, with a controller-friendly interface.
- **Friends and chat:** direct messages and group chats, voice calls, screen sharing and Remote Play.
- **Private by design:** communication is peer-to-peer and end-to-end encrypted. There is no central server and no account.
- **Watch and listen together:** shared video and music sessions with friends.
- **Game boost, recordings, achievements and trophies.**
- **Android companion app:** chat, calls and group voice from your phone.

## Status

NOVA is in active development. A public download will be available here soon.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
